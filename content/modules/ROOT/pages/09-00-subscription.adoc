= Suscripción MaaS

Una `MaaSModelRef` queda `Ready` con *MaaSSubscription* y *MaaSAuthPolicy* que la referencian.

== UI

. Dashboard OpenShift AI → *Models as a Service* → *Subscriptions*
. *Create subscription*
. Name: `qwen-authenticated`
. Groups: `system:authenticated`
. Model: el `MaaSModelRef` `qwen3-06b` (namespace `llm`)
. Rate limit: `100` tokens / `1m`
. *Create*

. *Authorization policies* → *Create policy*
. Name: `qwen-authenticated-access`
. Group: `system:authenticated`
. Mismos model refs

== Verificar

[source,bash]
----
oc get maassubscription -n models-as-a-service
oc get maasauthpolicy -n models-as-a-service
oc get maasmodelref -n llm -o wide
----

`MaaSModelRef` en fase `Ready`.
