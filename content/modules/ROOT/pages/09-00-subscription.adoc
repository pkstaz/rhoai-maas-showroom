= Suscripción MaaS

Una `MaaSModelRef` solo queda `Ready` si hay *MaaSSubscription* y *MaaSAuthPolicy* que la referencian (governance pairing).

== UI (preferido)

. Dashboard OpenShift AI → *Models as a Service* / *AI Hub* → *Subscriptions* (o *Governance*)
. *Create subscription*
. Name: `qwen-authenticated`
. Subjects / groups: `system:authenticated`
. Model: el `MaaSModelRef` de `qwen3-06b` (namespace `llm`)
. Rate limit: `100` tokens / `1m` (free)
. *Create*

Si hay pantalla aparte de *Authorization policies*:

. *Create policy*
. Name: `qwen-authenticated-access`
. Group: `system:authenticated`
. Mismos model refs

Si la UI crea solo la subscription, añade la auth policy con el YAML.

== Verificar

[source,bash]
----
oc get maassubscription -n models-as-a-service
oc get maasauthpolicy -n models-as-a-service
oc get maasmodelref -n llm -o wide
----

`MaaSModelRef` en fase `Ready`.
